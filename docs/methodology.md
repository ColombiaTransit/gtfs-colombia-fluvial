# Research methodology

## Evidence

Retain original source identifiers, dates, source URL, raw value and normalized value. Record verification separately for route existence, operation, timetable, landing site and fare. Suggested statuses: official_registered, official_current, operator_confirmed, locally_confirmed, historical, unverified, inactive. A missing operator website is not negative evidence.

## Source consolidation

The previous research conversation reports 500 general route registrations and two pairs of potentially duplicate thematic publications (272 passenger and 228 tourism records). These are provisional reported counts until reproduced against archived snapshots. Compare internal IDs, normalized attributes and geometry; retain source-specific IDs and conflicts. Do not discard separate operators just because geometry overlaps.

## Timetables

Daily is a service-day pattern, not a headway. Preserve the distinction between departure times, operating windows, alternating operators and on-demand service. Do not infer direction, travel duration, intermediate stops or holiday service without recording evidence or explicit assumptions.

## Geography

Match landing sites by name, municipality and spatial proximity; review ambiguous matches manually. DIMAR offshore pilot boarding points are not passenger stops. Commercial port concessions do not establish public passenger access.

## Publication

Check redistribution terms and personal data before committing raw sources to this public repository. Archive snapshots with checksums and provenance where permitted. Keep restricted originals outside the public repository. No automatic GTFS publication until regional readiness and validation.
