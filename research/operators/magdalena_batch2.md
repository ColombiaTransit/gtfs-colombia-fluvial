## Cootragam

[Supertransporte October 2025 decision, p. 4](https://www.supertransporte.gov.co/documentos/2025/octubre/NotificacionesDDP_20/20256400574901.pdf) cites Resolution 2023040011525 of 2023-03-17 and confirms all six corridors daily in both directions; Rio Viejo is explicitly included between Gamarra and El Banco. It does not give individual departure times. The operator reported approximately twelve vessels dispatched daily in June 2025. No newer renewal verified.

[Universidad Popular del Cesar study](https://repositorio.unicesar.edu.co/server/api/core/bitstreams/131ff191-3d0d-453a-ba28-a7d03c30a779/content), p. 57 and appendix A (interview 2025-09-24), describes a monthly rotating roster, 05:30–16:45 operating span and eight lines reported by the manager. Do not infer that these eight lines equal the six licensed corridors or copy the operating window to each route. Text inspected; screenshot timed out. The source includes additional destinations without route-level schedules. The semicolon in source time 8;00 is a probable typo, not silently corrected in CSV.

[Diario del Cesar, 2026-09-11](https://www.diariodelcesar.com/archivos/346833/amenazas-de-extorsion-obligan-a-suspender-ruta-fluvial/) reports indefinite suspension of Gamarra–Cerro Burgos due to extortion threats. No resumption confirmation found in targeted searches. Hold both directions from publication pending confirmation; no inference of suspension of other routes.

## Transportadora San Pablo Ltda

[Supertransporte Santander characterization, pp. 12–13](https://www.supertransporte.gov.co/documentos/2023/Febrero/Puertos_10/25.Santander.pdf) reports shared corridors, coordinated departure slots at Yuma and equal fares with Cootransfluviales. Actual departures may depend on passenger demand. This supports a joint research group, not automatic merging of all departures. Keep Transportadora San Pablo Ltda distinct from Transportes San Pablo S.A. (special service). The historical [national report](https://www.supertransporte.gov.co/documentos/2020/Agosto/Puertos_21/SITUACION-DE-LA-INFRAESTRUCTURA-Y-TRANSPORTE-FLUVIAL-EN-COLOMBIA.pdf) search excerpt references resolutions 3380 (2000), 728 (2011), 1494 (2014); originals not verified. [Tourism application notice](https://mintransporte.gov.co/loader.php?idFile=25582&lFuncion=exposeDocument&lServicio=Tools2&lTipo=descargas) was posted February 2021, not a 2026 passenger permit; text inspected, screenshot failed. It is an application, not an authorization. No newer passenger timetable or permit verified.
